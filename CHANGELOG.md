# Changelog

Creator Pack Editor, beta. Newest first. Each version here is a tag and a release on the beta repo.

## 0.1.4-beta (22 Nov 2026)

- The hook title never stops on a weak word like "very".

## 0.1.3-beta (22 Nov 2026)

- New layout: your video big in the middle on every step, steps down the side, chat at the bottom.
- Story as picture cards: your hook marked first, drag to reorder, switch takes, leave parts out, undo.
- Punch-in zooms at each cut, centred on your face, with an optional slow push on the hook.
- Your hook on screen at the start: Soft, Clean or Poster.
- Sideways video in an upright frame: Fill, Black bars (new default), Picture background or Blur. Words sit in the empty space.
- Shapes renamed: Full screen and Feed post.
- Change your footage and it listens again on its own.
- Export can also save a captions file (.srt).

## 0.1.2-beta (22 Nov 2026)

- Opening the page no longer makes an empty video each time.
- Videos with the same name show (2), (3) so you can tell them apart.
- Messages pop up at the top, never over your video or captions.
- If the pack is already open, it says so and uses another address.
- The timeline on Finish shows every part of your story.
- No more "Claude isn't listening" flash right after a new version.
- Plainer words for the word highlight and the Fix it button.

## 0.1.1-beta (22 Nov 2026)

- Four steps: Footage, Story, Look, Finish. Tidy up now sits inside Look.
- Caption looks: Soft (the default), Clean and Glow. No hard outlines.
- Where the words sit: top, middle, bottom, left, right, or moves around.
- Captions pop in as you say them (switch it off in Look).
- Colouring the word being said is now a choice, off by default.
- Export keeps your video on screen and shows where it was saved.
- Your videos menu: easier to read, rename any video.
- Story Studio teaser.

## 0.1.0-beta (22 Nov 2026)

First beta.

- Setup page: checks your Mac and installs what's missing (Python helpers, ffmpeg, the speech model).
- Editor: drop in your takes, pick what it's for, press Go. Claude Code builds the story from your own words; the page cuts out ums, pauses and repeats, adds captions and exports.
- Chat per video: ask for changes in plain words, every version is kept.
- Checks on every cut: missing words, lip sync, clipped sentences, marked on the timeline.
- Story Studio shows as coming next.
